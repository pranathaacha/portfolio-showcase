# Ceracon Cloud

Browser-based showroom operations

[Try the interactive demo](https://itspranathmaacha.wixsite.com/pranath-aacha/ceracon-cloud-demo)

*Reserve boxes at a godown, then dispatch the delivery. Watch physical and available stock update separately.*

Give showroom staff a shared view of what can be quoted, reserved, and delivered.

## My work

I’m developing the browser-based version of the showroom workflow, with quotations, stock reservations, trip-wise deliveries, and payment tracking. It is a separate product from the offline Windows application.
